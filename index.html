Before you get excited, the costs

Rent for a good two-bed runs roughly AED 140–220k a year. School fees are AED 40–90k per child. Health insurance is usually employer-paid; pensions are your own problem. For most senior packages the arithmetic still lands heavily in your favour, but run your own numbers, not ours. The full working, costs included, is in The maths of leaving.

No brochure

The honest bit

People who move on a brochure leave within a year. It’s in our interest, commercially as much as morally, to tell it straight.

Is the ecosystem real, or is it conference-stand hype?
Both exist. There is real capital deploying into real companies at DIFC, ADGM and Hub71. There is also noise. Part of what the network does is tell you which is which, company by company. We only run mandates for funded, licensed ventures.
What about the politics?
The UAE is not a liberal democracy and we won’t pretend otherwise. Speech, press and assembly work differently. Most members find daily professional life straightforward and safe; some things you’d say publicly in London you won’t say publicly there. If that trade-off is unacceptable to you, this isn’t your move. Genuinely.
Is this a one-way door for my career?
The strongest pattern we see is the opposite: bigger remit, faster capital, regional P&L earlier than London would ever give it to you. The risk isn’t going; it’s going to the wrong company. That’s the thing we exist to prevent.
And my family?
The move succeeds or fails at home, not at work. Schools, housing, spouse’s career, healthcare: we handle the logistics as part of every placement, and we’ll connect your partner with members’ partners who’ve done it. If the family maths doesn’t work, we’ll say so.
What does 25 North cost me?
Nothing. Companies pay us to find and land senior people; you never do. Our incentive is that you stay and thrive. Our fees carry a guarantee period, so a bad match costs us the fee.
